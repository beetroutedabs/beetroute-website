Privacy · Updated 30 October 2026

Privacy.

The short version: this site has no cookies, no tracking and no ads. Nothing you type into it reaches us until you choose to send it.

What you might share with us

If you send an enquiry or the Project Starter, it holds what you typed in: your name and email, and anything else you add, such as your phone number, your business, your budget and what you'd like to make.

How it reaches us

The forms don't send anything by themselves. They open your own email app with your message written in, and you press send. It arrives at beetrouted@gmail.com, a Gmail account, so Google stores those emails for us.

What stays on your device

So you can pick up where you left off, your draft enquiry and Project Starter answers are saved in your own browser, along with which hidden beets you've found. They never leave your device by themselves. To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

What we use it for

Only to reply to you, talk about your project and, if we work together, to do the work. We don't sell it, we don't share it for marketing, and we won't add you to a mailing list.

How long we keep it

For as long as we're talking about or working on your project, and afterwards only what the law asks us to keep, such as invoices. Ask us and we'll delete the rest.

Other services this site uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it sees visitors' IP addresses to deliver pages and keep them secure.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser fetches them from Google.

Your choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time. Email beetrouted@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.
